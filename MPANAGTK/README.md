Meal Planner and Nutrition Assistant (MPANA)

Overview

MPANA is a desktop application designed to help users plan healthy meals and gain insights into their nutritional intake.  It provides a user interface for browsing food composition data, selecting ingredients, viewing nutritional profiles, and calculating personalized health metrics like BMI and BMR.

MPANA aims to bridge the gap between raw food data and practical meal planning, by acting as a central hub, integrating diverse datasets related to food composition, culinary recipes, and food chemistry. This is achieved by correlate data aggregated from different sources to provide understanding of foods, recipe and meal plans. Linking individual ingredients to full recipes to understand the overall nutritional impact of a meal in addition to connecting dietary intake with personal health indicators like BMI and BMR. Food details and chemical makeups of food can be used to see connections between flavors, compounds, and effects

By establishing these connections, MPANA aims to guide users towards balanced and personalized dietary choices.

Features

    • Browse Food Data
      Explore a comprehensive database of foods with detailed nutritional information (sourced from the USDA).
    • Select Ingredients
      Easily select ingredients for meals and recipes.
    • View Nutritional Profiles
      See the combined nutritional content of selected ingredients, including macronutrients, vitamins, and minerals.
    • BMI/BMR Calculation
      Calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI) and Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) to understand your body's energy needs.
    • Food chemistry database
      access food chemistry data for enhanced food database understanding
      
Data Sources
       
    • USDA Food Composition Database (CSV)
    • CulinaryDB Recipe Data (CSV)

Technologies

    • C#
    • GTK# (for the user interface)
    • SQLite (for local data storage)
    • Entity Framework Core (for database access)
